## Limits of these numbers

* Weak, rule-derived labels: the headline macro-F1 is agreement with the labelling rules. Only the incident check and the blind model are independent of them.
* 34 incidents is a small sample; per-tier counts are single digits. Treat the recall figures as indications, not rates.
* One model, one seed, one split. The confidence intervals are block bootstraps over cells; they do not cover choice of split.
* Vegetation fires are not confirmed by burned-area data; GEM trackers are not loaded (see LABELS.md).
* `industrial_fire_candidate` has no training examples (the verified incidents are test-only), so the model cannot output it; an industrial fire shows up as a high P(industrial) with episodic behaviour and a large frp_z.
